# Hollow Dread

Psychological horror mod for Minecraft Fabric 1.21.1: a fear system, paranormal events, and a stalker that only moves when you're not looking.

## Features

**The Stalker:** a tall, pale creature that appears in the dark. It stands perfectly still while you stare at it and rushes you the moment you look away. Stare at it for too long and it vanishes with a scream.

**Fear system:** your fear rises when you stay in the dark or underground and drops in the light. The more frightened you are, the stranger things get: footsteps behind you, sounds that shouldn't exist, torches going out on their own, whispers in the chat, pulses of darkness, and a pounding heartbeat.

**Warding Charm:** hold it in your hand to calm your fear and drive the Stalker away.

## Commands (op)

- `/dread fear <0-1000>`
- `/dread event`
- `/dread spawn`

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric Loader, and Fabric API.
